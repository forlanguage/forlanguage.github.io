# M6.8D — Non-Listening High-Yield R4

## Goal

M6.8D deliberately pauses Listening expansion and concentrates the next content batch on Grammar/Vocabulary, Reading, Writing and Speaking. The release is driven by the user's own practice results, error tracker, knowledge map, recent exam-review notes and personalised language collections.

## Source-derived priority

The source material gives a clear order of work:

- Practice 01: Reading 24/29 (82.8%), Grammar 12/25 (48.0%), Vocabulary 24/25 (96.0%). Grammar is the largest accuracy gap.
- Error Patterns: repeated errors are modal perfect/deduction, mixed conditionals, wish, modal perfect passive, negative past possibility and Reading text cohesion.
- Knowledge Map: Priority 1 includes adjectives/adverbs, future continuous, modal perfect, mixed conditionals and wish; Priority 2 includes narrative tenses, reported structures, phrasal verbs, Reading cohesion and collocations.
- Recent-review notes identify useful question-pattern signals for Speaking and Writing: short personal questions, picture description, two-picture comparison, choice/reason questions, club forms, short personal responses, chat-room discussion and informal/formal emails after a club-plan or service change.
- Common English RYAN supplies useful learner language patterns such as `express my concerns`, `may cause difficulties`, `cover the operating costs`, `suggest + V-ing`, `play an important role in`, and technology/classroom comparison themes.

These sources are used as a **priority and pattern map**, not as a licence to reconstruct a live examination.

## Publication boundary

- No recalled live-test prompt is copied verbatim.
- No collected JPEG from the user's notes folder is republished because redistribution rights are not established.
- New Speaking images are original ForLanguage SVG artwork released as project CC0-1.0 assets.
- Questions inspired by a recurring theme are independently authored with new wording, details, distractors and response framing.
- Canonical Core/Reading counts remain unchanged; supplemental drills are separately identified as High-Yield Practice.

## High-Yield Practice

New release: `HY-2026.08-R1`

Path: `/aptis/targeted/`

Bank:

- 30 targeted Grammar MCQ;
- 10 targeted Vocabulary MCQ;
- 4 Reading Part 2 text-cohesion sets;
- 2 Reading Part 3 opinion/paraphrase sets.

Grammar clusters:

1. Modal perfect and deduction;
2. Mixed conditionals and wish;
3. Future and narrative tenses;
4. Reported speech, reporting passive and passive structures;
5. Adjective/adverb, comparative modifiers and phrasal verbs.

Reading Part 2 deliberately emphasises local cohesion: noun → pronoun, cause → result, preparation → action → outcome, and chronological markers such as `before`, `once`, `by`, `at the end` and `since then`.

Reading Part 3 focuses on paraphrase rather than keyword copying. It remains a maintenance skill because the source tracker already shows relatively strong performance in opinion matching.

## Writing R4

Release: `WR-2026.08-R4`

Adds WT16–WT20, taking the runtime to 20 full tests / 100 task records.

R4 themes:

- social meetings for different age groups;
- garden club weekend project;
- music and arts event changes;
- sports club facilities;
- study and skills seminar.

The main pattern is more important than any single topic: Part 1 short form response → Part 2 20–30 words → Part 3 three reasoned responses of 30–40 words → Part 4 informal 40–50 words plus formal 120–150 words, often responding to a change in schedule, service, fee or delivery mode.
