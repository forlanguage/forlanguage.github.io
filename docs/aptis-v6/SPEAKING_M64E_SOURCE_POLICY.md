# M6.4E Speaking source and editorial policy

## Authoritative basis

The public British Council Aptis Speaking preparation pages are used as the authoritative source for test structure and timings:

- Aptis Speaking has four sections and takes about 12 minutes.
- Part 1 contains three personal questions, with 30 seconds for each response.
- Part 2 uses one photograph followed by three responses: description, personal experience and a broader opinion; each response is 45 seconds.
- Part 3 uses two photographs followed by three responses: description/comparison, comparison and opinion/speculation; each response is 45 seconds.
- Part 4 presents three questions on one abstract topic, gives one minute of preparation and allows a two-minute response.

Source pages:

- https://www.britishcouncil.org/exam/aptis/take-test/speaking-video
- https://www.britishcouncil.org/exam/english/aptis/prepare-general
- https://www.britishcouncil.org/exam/english/aptis/research/publications/technical/general-technical-manual-version-2-2

## Copyright boundary

British Council materials are used for format verification only. The ForLanguage bank does not copy official practice-test prompts, photographs or candidate materials. All published prompts in `bank-v2.json` are newly written for ForLanguage, and all image assets are original vector illustrations released by ForLanguage under CC0-1.0.

The bank must therefore be described as **British Council format-aligned original practice**, not as an official British Council test bank.

## Editorial review rules

A full test must contain exactly ten recorded responses:

- Part 1: three tasks, 30 seconds each, no required image.
- Part 2: three tasks, 45 seconds each, all referencing the same single image.
- Part 3: three tasks, 45 seconds each, all referencing the same pair of images.
- Part 4: one task, 60 seconds preparation and 120 seconds response; the prompt contains three related questions.

Every published test must include:

- original-source declaration;
- British Council format reference;
- internal editorial reviewer metadata;
- coherent topic progression;
- image source, licence, attribution, alt text and SHA-256 checksum;
- no remembered, leaked or copied examination material.

## Review status

M6.4E uses structured internal editorial review. It is not independent external examiner review and does not establish official Aptis equivalence.
