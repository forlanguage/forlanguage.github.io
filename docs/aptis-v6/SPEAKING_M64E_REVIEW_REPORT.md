## Authoritative format basis

The release structure follows the public British Council description of Aptis General / Aptis ESOL General Speaking:

- Part 1: three personal questions, 30 seconds each.
- Part 2: one photograph, three questions, 45 seconds each.
- Part 3: two photographs, three questions, 45 seconds each.
- Part 4: three linked questions, one minute preparation and a two-minute response.

References:

- British Council Aptis Speaking test practice page.
- British Council Prepare for Aptis ESOL General page.
- British Council Aptis General Technical Manual page.

## Copyright and provenance decision

No official British Council prompt, photograph or practice-test item is copied into the repository. British Council sources are used to verify structure, timing and task progression only.

All prompts were written specifically for ForLanguage. All SVG prompt images were created for this release and declared under `CC0-1.0`. Therefore, the correct release description is:

> Original ForLanguage practice content aligned to the publicly documented British Council Aptis General Speaking format.

It must not be described as an official British Council bank.

## Editorial checks completed

- Exactly 10 responses per full test.
- Correct response times for Parts 1–4.
- Part 2 reuses one image across all three questions.
- Part 3 reuses one image pair across all three questions.
- Part 4 includes three linked questions and the correct preparation time.
- Stable test and task IDs.
- Topic, rubric and image references resolve.
- Every test includes source and reviewer metadata.
- Every task includes an editorial review note.
- Public bank contains only `PUBLISHED_FINAL` records.
- Prompts are distinct across the five tests.
- Images include alt text, source, licence, attribution and checksum metadata.

## Review limitations

The review is a structured internal editorial review. It is not an independent review by a British Council examiner, and it does not prove official Aptis score equivalence or psychometric validity.
