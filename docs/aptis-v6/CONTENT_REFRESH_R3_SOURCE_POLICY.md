# Content Refresh R3 — source policy and bank audit

## Purpose

This release expands the ForLanguage Aptis General practice bank while keeping a strict distinction between authoritative format references, user-provided review signals, and publishable original practice content.

## Source hierarchy

1. **British Council Aptis ESOL General Candidate Guide (2023)** — authoritative public reference for task types, timings, response lengths, and Listening two-play behaviour.
2. **British Council Aptis General Practice Tests (2020)** — public sample/practice reference used to confirm the 17-task Listening progression and the structure of Core, Reading, Writing, Speaking and Listening.
3. **British Council–EAQUALS Core Inventory for General English** — B2 language-function, grammar, discourse, vocabulary and topic coverage reference. It is a curriculum/teaching inventory, not an Aptis item bank.
4. **Aptis B2 Learning Graph Question Bank – 2696 with 24 Reading Tests v5** — current ForLanguage Core/Reading source bank.
5. **User-provided recent reviews, popular-question notes and personalised Speaking/Writing collections** — used only to identify recurring themes and useful practice domains. They are not treated as official questions or answer keys.

## Publication rule

- Do not copy official British Council prompts, recordings, passages, photographs or sample tests into the public ForLanguage bank unless a separate licence explicitly allows redistribution.
- Do not publish a recalled/reported live-test question verbatim.
- A recalled topic may be converted into a new practice scenario only after the prompt, details, answer choices and wording are independently authored.
- Images inspired by topic categories are redrawn as original ForLanguage vector artwork; R3 images are released as CC0-1.0 project artwork.
- Every new Writing/Speaking/Listening item carries provenance or review metadata.

## Current bank audit

### Core

The existing learning-graph source contains 1,000 Grammar and 1,000 Vocabulary items. Its QA sheet reports zero answer-schema errors and zero duplicate answer-option errors, but it also records **332 exact repeated Grammar prompts** and **335 repeated Vocabulary prompts within subtype**. Those repetitions were intentionally retained for personal drilling in the source bank.

R3 does **not** increase the Core count. A later quality batch should prioritise prompt diversity, semantic near-duplicate detection, distractor strength, answer-position balance and B2 concept coverage rather than raw item count.

### Reading

The current source reports 24 complete tests, 120 task groups and 696 scored items. Reading QA reports all 24 tests passing the four-part / 29-answer / 35-minute structural check and marks all 696 items as low ambiguity. R3 therefore leaves Reading quantity unchanged and treats it as an audit/quality-maintenance target rather than an expansion target.

### Writing

R3 adds WT11–WT15, taking the public runtime from 10 to 15 full tests. The five new tests use original prompts around science exhibitions, club services, community transport, technology events and environmental volunteering. Public British Council word limits are retained: Part 2 20–30 words, Part 3 30–40 words per response, Part 4 informal 40–50 and formal 120–150.

### Speaking

R3 adds ST06–ST10, taking the public runtime from 5 to 10 full tests. The new content covers sport/family/effort, travel/culture, technology/learning, arts/media and community environmental action. Seven new original SVG images are added. The four-part timing contract remains 3×30 s, 3×45 s, 3×45 s, then 60 s preparation + 120 s response.

### Listening

Listening is the largest content gap. R3/Listening Batch 1 adds two original full tests, LT03 and LT04. Each contains:

- Part 1: 13 information-recognition tasks;
- Part 2: 1 four-speaker information-matching task with six options;
- Part 3: 1 Man/Woman/Both opinion-matching task with four statements;
- Part 4: 2 monologue-comprehension tasks with two questions each;
- 17 tasks and 25 scored answer slots in total;
- maximum two plays per task.

The current production limitation is audio. The public player still uses browser TTS when `audio_url` is empty. The scripts and questions are publishable original practice content, but TTS is only a functional fallback and should not be described as exam-quality audio.

## Claims boundary

R3 content is **ForLanguage original practice material aligned to the publicly documented Aptis ESOL General format**. It is not an official British Council question bank, not a reconstruction of a live examination, and has not undergone British Council psychometric validation or independent examiner certification.
